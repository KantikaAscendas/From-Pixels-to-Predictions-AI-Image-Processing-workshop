- Files: `Lab01_Nuclei/data/lymph_node_roi.png` (a crop of the full-resolution level of `patient_000_node_1.tif`, region x 0.53–0.55 cm, y 2.064–2.08 cm) and `wsi_overview_level9.png` (the lowest-resolution level of the same slide).
